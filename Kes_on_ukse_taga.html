Otsustage, kumb on A ja kumb B. Esimeses voorus küsib A. Öelge küsimused ja vastused valjusti. Kui jätkuküsimust on raske moodustada, veeretage uuesti.

Choose who is A and who is B. Start with A as the asker. Say each question and answer aloud before moving on. If the follow-up prompt is hard to use, roll it again.
